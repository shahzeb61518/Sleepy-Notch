Which phones does it work on?

Android phones with a punch-hole or notch camera at the top of the screen.

Does it use my camera?

No. The eyes are drawn beside the camera hole. The camera is never opened.

Why does it ask to display over other apps?

That's how the eyes stay visible while you use other apps. Your taps still go to the app underneath.

The eyes disappeared after a while. What can I do?

Some phones stop background apps to save battery. In Sleepy Notch, tap Battery and set it to Unrestricted. On Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones, also allow Autostart and Pop-ups in background from the app.

Why is there a notification?

Android requires one while an app keeps something on screen. You can turn the eyes off right from it.
