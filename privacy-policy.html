Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Sleepy Notch draws two animated eyes beside your phone's front camera. They get sleepy as the bedtime you choose gets close.

What we collect

Nothing. Sleepy Notch does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or device data.

  • The app has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, ads or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not use the camera. The eyes are drawn beside the camera hole; the camera is never opened.
  • It does not read your screen, your touches, your notifications or your apps.

What the app looks at on your phone

To make the eyes react, the app checks a few things on the phone. None of it is saved or leaves the phone.

  • The clock, and whether the screen is on.
  • The battery level and whether the phone is charging.
  • Whether headphones are connected.
  • How the phone is tilted, from the motion sensor, so the eyes can glance when you tip it. This only runs while the screen is on.
  • To suggest a usual bedtime, which country the phone is in, from the mobile network, time zone or language setting. It doesn't use your location.

Your settings

Your settings (whether the eyes are on, your bedtime and wake-up times (including weekend times), the eye style and colour, the name you give the eyes, your accessory, your birthday if you add it, and which reactions are on) are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Sharing

"Share my eyes" makes a short video on your phone. It's only sent where you choose to share it, using your phone's share menu.
