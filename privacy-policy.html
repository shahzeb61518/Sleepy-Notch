Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Sleepy Notch draws two animated eyes beside your phone's front camera. They get sleepy as the bedtime you choose gets close.

What we collect

Nothing. Sleepy Notch does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or device data.

  • The app has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, ads or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not use the camera. The eyes are drawn beside the camera hole; the camera is never opened.
  • It does not read your screen, your touches or your apps. It only reads the phone's clock and whether the screen is on.
  • To suggest a usual bedtime, it checks which country the phone is in, from the mobile network, time zone or language setting. It doesn't use your location, and this never leaves the phone.

Your settings (whether the eyes are on, your bedtime and wake-up time, the eye style, and whether yawning is on) are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
